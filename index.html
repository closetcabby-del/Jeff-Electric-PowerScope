HOW TO PLAY

Spot it. Jump it. Learn it.

  1. Press Space, Arrow Up, tap the game or use the large Jump button.
  2. Jump over electrical bad ideas and collect electric-gold bolts.
  3. If the van reaches a hazard, the game pauses and explains the safe homeowner takeaway.

This game teaches recognition only. It does not inspect, diagnose or replace emergency services, the utility, a landlord or a licensed electrician.
