TRANSPARENCY

Data & Methodology

PowerScope combines published public statistics with clearly labeled regional educational context. It does not use parcel records, inspect a home or make a property-level finding.

Primary sources

  • U.S. Energy Information Administration — Electric Sales, Revenue and Average Price
  • U.S. Fire Administration — Residential Building Fire Causes
  • ERCOT — Yearly Peak Demand Records
  • U.S. Census American Community Survey

How to read this experience

Map overlays are illustrative regional visualizations around supported ZIP centroids. Home-age notes describe broad community development patterns, not ACS parcel-level values. Storm markers are historical regional context and do not represent a utility outage record. Readiness points are intentionally non-electrical units and must not be used to size service.

Retrieval date

Source pages and final annual data reviewed July 30, 2026. Exact chart values and citations are documented in data.js.
