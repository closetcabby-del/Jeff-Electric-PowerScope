BEFORE YOU BEGIN

Observe. Don’t investigate.

  • Never remove a panel, switch or outlet cover.
  • Never touch exposed conductors or test live wiring.
  • Never disassemble an outlet, connection or appliance.
  • Keep away from downed lines, damaged meters and electrical equipment near water.

Active smoke, fire, arcing or immediate danger: leave the area and call 911. This guide does not replace emergency services, your utility or a licensed electrician.
