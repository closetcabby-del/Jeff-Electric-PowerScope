SAFE EXPLORATION

Observe. Don’t investigate.

  • Never remove a panel, switch or outlet cover.
  • Never touch exposed conductors or test live wiring.
  • Never disassemble electrical equipment or perform energized work.
  • Stay away from downed lines, damaged meters and electrical equipment near water.

Active smoke, fire, arcing or immediate danger: leave the area and call 911. This experience does not replace emergency services, your utility or a licensed electrician.
