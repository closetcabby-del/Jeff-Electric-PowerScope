    BEFORE YOU BEGIN

    Observe. Don’t investigate.

    • Never remove an electrical panel or outlet cover.
    • Never touch exposed wiring or test live equipment.
    • Never disassemble an outlet, switch or appliance connection.
    • Stay away from downed lines, damaged meters and water near energized equipment.

    Active smoke, fire, arcing or immediate danger: leave the area and call 911. This guide does not replace emergency services, a utility or a licensed electrician.
